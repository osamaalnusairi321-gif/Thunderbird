BEFORE WE MAKE PLANS

A few things
you might ask.

What kinds of events can I host?+

Weddings, receptions, birthdays, baby showers, family reunions, corporate events, and community gatherings. Have something else in mind? Talk to Kara about your idea.

Can I see the space first?+

Call or email Kara to ask about a venue visit and talk through the space for your event.

How do I check dates and pricing?+

Share your preferred date, event type, and expected guest count with Kara. She can confirm availability, rental pricing, and the details for your gathering.

Is there indoor and outdoor space?+

Yes. Thunderbird has an indoor gathering space and two acres outdoors. Ask Kara about guest capacity, parking, and how each area could work for your event.
