THUNDERBIRD EVENT SPACE WEBSITE

To view on your Mac:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html.

Files:
- index.html: website content
- styles.css: layout and design
- script.js: menu and photo gallery
- assets/: venue photos and logo

To upload to GitHub or a web host, keep these files and the assets folder together. index.html should be at the top level of your website folder. No installation or build step is needed.

Phone and email buttons open the visitor's phone or email application. They do not send messages automatically.

The preview includes a noindex tag. Remove the robots meta tag in index.html when launching the official website.
